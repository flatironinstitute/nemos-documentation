Citation Guide and Bibliography#

If you use NeMoS in a published academic article or presentation, please cite the code by the DOI. You can use the following:

  • Code:

Additionally, it may be a good idea to cite the following paper(s) depending on which component you use:

  • GLM or PopulationGLM: Nelder and Wedderburn, 1972.

  • BSplineEval or BSplineConv: Cox, 1972, De Boor, 1972.

  • MSplineEval or MSplineConv: Ramsay, 1988.

  • RaisedCosineLinearEval or RaisedCosineLogEval or RaisedCosineLinearConv or RaisedCosineLogConv: Pillow et al., 2005.

  • ElasticNet: Zou and Hastie, 2005.

  • GroupLasso: Yuan and Lin, 2006.

  • Lasso: Tibshirani, 1996.

  • Ridge: Hoerl and Kennard, 1970, Hoerl and Kennard, 1970.
